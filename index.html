VITA-AD3ECK-20%

1000 ml

A vitamin supplement containing Vitamins A, D3, E, K3 and C to support growth, immunity, reproduction and overall animal health.

View Product Details

Composition per 1 Liter

  • Vitamin A — 20,000,000 IU
  • Vitamin D3 — 25,000,000 IU
  • Vitamin E — 15,000 mg
  • Vitamin K3 — 10,000 mg
  • Vitamin C — 10,000 mg
  • Excipients and deionized water up to 1000 ml

Benefits

  • Supports normal growth and reproduction.
  • Supports healthy skin and epithelial tissues.
  • Supports calcium and phosphorus utilization.
  • Supports bone development and eggshell formation.
  • Vitamin E provides antioxidant support.
  • Vitamin C supports animals during heat stress.
  • Vitamin K3 supports normal blood clotting.

Indications

  • Vitamin deficiency.
  • Maintaining poultry health and production.
  • Supporting fertility and immunity.

Dosage

  • Poultry: 1 ml / 10 L drinking water for 3-5 days.
  • Cows & Horses: 1 ml / 10 kg body weight for 3-5 days.
  • Sheep & Goats: 1 ml / 10 kg body weight for 3-5 days.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight or heat, at approximately 30°C.

Withdrawal Period: Zero

View Product Details

Benefits
